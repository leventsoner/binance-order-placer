Asset Free Locked
Sell: stop below market price. Buy: stop above market price. Stop Limit places a limit order at Price when triggered; OCO places it at Stop Limit Price.
OCO only: the stop leg's limit price. Price is the other leg's take-profit limit, and Time In Force applies to the stop leg.
Optional, Limit and Market orders only. Fill in both or neither. Limit: once the order fully fills, Binance places a take-profit limit at Take Profit Price and a stop-market order at Stop Loss Price for the same quantity. Market: after the order fills, a take-profit limit and stop-market pair is placed for the filled quantity (less any fee taken in the bought coin). When one executes, the other is cancelled. For Market these are two separate requests: if the second is rejected, the order stays filled without TP/SL and the error says so.
